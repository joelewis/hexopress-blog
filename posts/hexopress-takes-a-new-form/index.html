HexoPress Takes a New Form

March 21, 2026

Roughly a decade back, I built HexoPress to scratch my itch. Back then my writings were scattered over Wordpress, custom-built blog engines, Svbtle, Gists and few other places (that I don’t even remember!).

It took a while but eventually I realized my writings should live somewhere more permanent. I found Google Docs has always been my thinking pad. Luckily Google Docs is hard to kill (Let’s see how this statement ages in coming decades) and a great place to dump my writings. So I built HexoPress to take my writings from Google Docs and publish a blogging site out of it. It was fantastic and I had a great run with it :)

I even published it as a SaaS software (not live anymore) and put it up on HN. HN loved it and hugged it to front page not once but twice, a few years apart!

Anyway life caught on. I slowly stopped publishing. HexoPress wasn’t needed anymore. And it eventually faded into the abyss.

Well, now here we are in the new world dominated by AI. Most writings are now AI written. Heck, most softwares are now AI written!

I’ve revived HexoPress to live in this new world. One where SaaS is a joke. HexoPress really is just a tool. A small local software that syncs between two platforms. Regardless of the hate AI gets, I like how AI has made file formats and small programs cool again.

So here goes: HexoPress is now published as a cli tool that you can use with your AI. My goal is to expose HexoPress as a simple cli script and package it as a Skill for others to use it via their own AI tool.
